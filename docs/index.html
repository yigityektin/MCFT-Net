Sparse-reference video super-resolution

Recover detail from motion, frequency, and a few real HR frames.

MCFT-Net combines deterministic motion-compensated frequency transfer with a confidence-guided refinement CNN for video restoration with sparse high-resolution anchors.

Explore the modelRead the code
The idea

A two-stage refinement framework

The deterministic stage propagates information from sparse anchors. The learned stage predicts conservative corrections while preserving the base candidate and the low-resolution observation.

01 · Motion compensation

Estimate motion and align anchor evidence to the target frame.

02 · Frequency transfer

Transfer reliable high-frequency structure into a constrained MCFT v3 candidate.

03 · Confidence refinement

A lightweight CNN learns where and how strongly to refine the candidate.
